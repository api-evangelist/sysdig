Sysdig Automations unifies fragmented cloud security workflows by connecting detection, investigation, and response into a single, cohesive system, reducing inefficiencies and manual effort. It enables teams to automate actions based on risks, vulnerabilities, threats, and runtime events, ensuring faster, more consistent responses while integrating seamlessly with existing tools. By consolidating workflows within one platform, organizations can improve operational efficiency, reduce tool sprawl, and accelerate response times in increasingly complex cloud environments.
