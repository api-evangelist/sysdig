Organizations operate across SaaS, private cloud, on-premises, and air-gapped environments, each shaped by specific regulatory, operational, and connectivity constraints. Sysdig Secure supports these deployment models with a common runtime security foundation, while adapting how capabilities are delivered and operated in each environment. This allows teams to maintain control, meet compliance requirements, and effectively secure modern workloads within their existing infrastructure.
